Honest Assessment

What AI Can vs. Can't Catch

The unfiltered truth about AI-augmented auditing. No sugarcoating.

✓ AI Catches (30-50% of Medium)

  • Complex reentrancy across multiple functions
  • First-depositor / inflation attacks
  • Frontrunning & sandwich vulnerabilities
  • Precision loss in math operations
  • Storage collision in proxy patterns
  • Missing access control in role systems
  • Speed: 10,000 lines in under 10 minutes
  • Coverage: 100% of functions vs 60-80% human

✗ AI Misses (The $10M+ Bugs)

  • Protocol-specific business logic errors
  • Economic exploits & incentive misalignment
  • Oracle manipulation (TWAP gaming, multi-block MEV)
  • Flash loan chains across 3+ protocols
  • Game-theoretic exploits & governance attacks
  • Novel attack vectors never seen before
  • False positive rate: 70-85%
  • % of real exploits caught: 10-20% of criticals

The Critical Insight

AI is table stakes, not an edge. Everyone has access to the same LLMs. The edge is speed + coverage + volume while you build the domain expertise that actually matters. Use AI for triage, not for thinking. The pipeline lets you process MORE code faster — enter every contest, cover every function — while your human brain focuses on the 200 lines that actually matter.
