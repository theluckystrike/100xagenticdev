## 1. Hacker News

**Why it works:** Developer-dense audience, zero cost, massive spike potential. A front-page post delivers 10,000-50,000 visitors in 24 hours. Developer tools convert at 3-8% from HN traffic.

### Getting to the Front Page

**The algorithm:** You need 8-10 genuine upvotes and 2-3 thoughtful comments in the first 30 minutes to reach the top 10. After that, the algorithm takes over.

**Timing:** Tuesday-Thursday 8:00-10:00 AM Pacific (before engineering standups). Sunday 6:00-9:00 PM Pacific for low competition.

**What to post:**
- "Show HN" posts for launches (direct product link + description)
- Technical blog posts explaining how you built something
- Interesting technical findings or data analyses
- Open source project announcements

**Critical rules:**
- NEVER ask for upvotes. HN's ring-detection is aggressive. Shared-office IPs, voting circles, and Twitter "please upvote" links are auto-filtered and will get you penalized.
- Post a first comment as the author immediately: brief intro, backstory, what you are looking for, anticipated Q&A answers.
- Reply to every comment within 10 minutes. Early threads keep the story hot.
- Your content must be genuinely interesting. Trying to game HN without substance is a waste of time.

**Seed your early upvotes from:** Email list of technical subscribers who use HN, private Slack/Discord channels with members who have HN accounts over 1 year old.

**Expected results per front-page hit:**
- 10,000-50,000 page views
- 300-4,000 signups (at 3-8% conversion for dev tools)
- HN posts have long-tail: archived threads get Google traffic for years

### Repeatable Strategy

You can post to HN multiple times. Space it out by 2-4 weeks between posts. Each post should have a different angle:
1. Launch announcement ("Show HN: I built X")
2. Technical deep-dive ("How X handles Y at scale")
3. Retrospective with numbers ("X months in: lessons from building Z")
4. Open source announcement

## 2. Crypto Twitter / X

**Why it works:** Crypto Twitter is the real-time discovery layer for the entire crypto ecosystem. It is where narratives form, projects get discovered, and communities coalesce. For crypto products, this is the single most important channel.

### Growth Strategy (0 to 5,000 followers)

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Foundation**
- Post 2-3 times daily: mix of original insights, quote-tweets with analysis, replies to larger accounts
- Content that works: educational threads explaining protocols/tokenomics, memes that connect with crypto culture, charts and infographics with clear data, real project milestone updates
- Reply to 20-30 relevant tweets daily with substantive technical insight (not "great thread!")

**Phase 2 (Weeks 3-6): Threads and Build-in-Public**
- Write 1-2 long threads per week (8-15 tweets) breaking down technical concepts
- Share your building journey: screenshots, code snippets, architecture decisions
- Engage with threads from accounts with 10K-50K followers in your niche

**Phase 3 (Weeks 6-12): Amplification**
- Partner with niche influencers (20K followers with strong engagement > 100K with weak engagement)
- A small creator with 20,000 loyal followers can drive better results than a large account with weak engagement
- Engage with Spaces (Twitter's audio feature) -- join, contribute, eventually host

**Key insight for 2026:** Pure broadcast fails. The community-first layered approach produces durable growth. Projects that reply with technical insight or honest takes earn community standing. Builders want roadmaps, code updates, and technical clarity.

### Cost

$0 in direct costs. Time investment is 10-15 hours/week. Paid promotion via influencers costs $500-5,000 per campaign but is optional and often unnecessary for genuine technical products.

## 3. Product Hunt

**Why it works:** Concentrated burst of tech-savvy early adopters. A #1 Product of the Day badge becomes permanent social proof. PH is a 6-week project, not a 1-day event.

### The 6-Week Launch Playbook

**Weeks 1-4 (Pre-launch):**
- Build a waitlist of 500-1,000 followers on Product Hunt before launch day
- Engage on PH: upvote/comment on other products daily (build karma)
- Collect 50+ testimonials, beta user quotes, and screenshots
- Pre-write all launch copy, prepare GIF/video demo (under 60 seconds)

**Week 5 (Launch prep):**
- Recruit a well-known Hunter to submit your product (increases visibility)
- Queue 200+ first-hour supporters: email your waitlist, DM your network, schedule tweets
- Prepare personalized reply templates for common comment themes

**Launch Day (Week 6):**
- Launch at 12:01 AM PST on Tuesday or Wednesday (lowest competition, highest engaged audience)
- Critical window: 6-9 AM PT. Target 50+ upvotes/hour during this window.
- Reply to every comment personally within 15 minutes
- In 2026, engagement signals (comments, maker replies, time-on-page) matter more than raw upvote counts
- The algorithm rewards products that bring new users to PH, not just mobilize existing ones

**Post-launch (48 hours after):**
- Send personalized DMs to your top 30 commenters (90 minutes of work, 30% conversion rate vs. 2% for generic emails)
- Write a "lessons learned" blog post while you still have momentum
- Cross-post results to Twitter, Reddit, Indie Hackers

### Expected Results

- Product of the Day: 1,000-3,000 website visits on launch day
- Consumer apps: 1-3% conversion rate
- Developer tools: 3-8% conversion rate
- Total: 30-240 signups from a successful launch

## 4. GitHub (Open Source)

**Why it works:** GitHub has its own discovery algorithm. Stars drive search ranking, Trending page placement, and Explore feed features. Developers trust open source. Stars compound over time if the project shows health.

### The 9 Levers That Compound

1. **Clarity Above the Fold:** Answer what, who, and why in 5 seconds of scrolling. One-line description + GIF/screenshot + install command.

2. **README Optimization:** Structure as: positioning statement, visual demo, use-case bullets, quick-start (3 commands), social proof (star count, badges, testimonials), contributing guide.

3. **Wave-Based Launches:** Three sequential pushes:
   - Wave 1: Warm support (existing network, Discord, Slack)
   - Wave 2: Public distribution (HN "Show HN", Reddit, Twitter)
   - Wave 3: Follow-up content (blog post about the tech, comparison articles)

4. **Single Primary Channel Per Push:** Focus momentum on one platform per wave. Do not dilute.

5. **Fast Issue Response:** Reply to issues/PRs within 12 hours. This is visible social proof of maintainer quality.

6. **Convert Spikes to Search Assets:** After each launch spike, write comparison pages, tutorials, FAQs, and implementation guides.

7. **Broader Growth Loop:** Launch -> conversion -> blog content -> docs -> social proof -> more stars.

8. **Targeted Content:** One article per winning angle (setup guides, "X vs. Y" comparisons, templates).

9. **Visible Project Health:** Recent commits, answered issues, updated roadmap, fresh examples.

### Growth Benchmarks

- Median projects: 100 stars in 5 days, 1,000 stars in 31 days (for projects that get initial traction)
- Investors and users check your star growth curve -- smooth multi-country distribution is more credible than a single-week spike
- AFFiNE case study: reached 60K+ stars using this systematic approach

## 5. Reddit

**Why it works:** Reddit has massive crypto-native communities (r/cryptocurrency: 7M+, r/defi, r/ethfinance, r/CryptoTechnology). Reddit users are research-oriented and will deeply evaluate products. Reddit ad CPM is 29% lower and CPC is 41% lower than industry averages.

### Organic Strategy (The 70/30 Rule)

Spend 70% of effort on organic community building, 30% on paid once credibility is established.

**Content that works:**
- Lead with a topic, not a pitch. Thoughtful discussions earn organic traction; promotional posts get removed.
- DeFi subreddits prize analytical content and protocol comparisons
- Educational content about sector challenges with organic mentions of your solution
- Detailed technical breakdowns with data
- AMA posts once you have some traction

**Trust building:**
- Reddit remembers how a team behaves. Not just the launch thread, but follow-up replies, AMA answers, missed-timeline explanations, and how criticism gets handled.
- Stay visible after excitement cools.
- Build account karma before posting about your project (minimum 2-4 weeks of genuine participation).

**Expected results:**
- Reddit posts: 5-15% signup rate across targeted communities
- Cold email equivalent: 100 genuine Reddit interactions = 5-15 quality signups
- B2B SaaS on Reddit: 5-15% conversion rate (highest of any launch platform)

## 6. Telegram

**Why it works for crypto specifically:** 900M+ monthly active users as of 2026. The TON blockchain is deeply integrated into Telegram's commercial platform. Telegram Ads deliver crypto audiences at $0.50-$3 CPC (10-20x cost advantage over other platforms).

### Viral Mechanics

- **Tap-to-earn games** proved Telegram's viral distribution power: Hamster Kombat, Notcoin, and similar projects acquired millions of users through in-app viral mechanics
- **Mini Apps (TWAs):** Build directly inside Telegram. Users never leave the app. Massively reduces friction.
- **Group/Channel virality:** Create a public channel for announcements + a community group for discussion. Enable invite links with tracking.

### Growth Tactics

1. Create a Telegram Mini App if your product can function as one (TON integration available)
2. Run a bot that provides utility (price alerts, portfolio tracking, notifications) and cross-promotes your main product
3. Telegram Ads: $0.50-$3 per click, highly targeted to crypto audiences
4. Cross-promote in relevant crypto Telegram groups (with permission/partnership, not spam)
5. Use invite contests: reward existing members for bringing verified new members

### Expected Results

- 300-2,000 group members in 90 days for a crypto project with consistent effort
- Telegram Ads at $1 CPC with 10% conversion = $10 CAC (very competitive for crypto)

## 7. App Stores (Chrome Web Store, VS Code Marketplace)

**Why it works:** Built-in discovery algorithm acts like SEO you do not have to earn over years. The Chrome Web Store hosts ~178,000 extensions; only 0.2% cross 1M users. AI extensions are the fastest-growing segment (442 AI extensions with 1,000+ users in Jan 2026, up from 238 the prior year).

### Chrome Web Store

- Most extension installs come from CWS search, not external traffic
- The algorithm weights ratings heavily -- actively ask early users for 5-star reviews
- Optimize listing: title keywords, description, screenshots, demo video
- AI-powered extensions are in a growth sweet spot in 2026
- Cost: $5 one-time developer registration fee

### VS Code Marketplace

- Lower competition than Chrome Web Store
- Developer tools get organic discovery from VS Code's built-in extension browser
- Good README + demo GIF + clear value proposition = installs
- Synergizes with GitHub: link your extension to your open source repo

### Growth Playbook

1. Build the simplest useful version of your tool as an extension
2. Launch in the store with optimized listing (keywords, screenshots, video)
3. Cross-promote: HN post, Reddit post, Twitter thread about the extension
4. Ask every user for a review (in-extension prompt after 7 days of use)
5. Update frequently -- stores reward active development

**Expected results:** 200-1,000 users in 90 days. The store continues to drive organic installs indefinitely as a compounding asset.

## 8. Discord

**Why it works:** Highest retention of any channel. Discord communities create genuine relationship with users who become evangelists. For crypto projects, Discord is the standard community hub.

### Building from Zero

**Foundation (Week 1):**
- Keep channels minimal: #start-here, #rules, #announcements, #general, #support, one topical forum
- Add channels only when clear need appears (fewer decisions = higher new-member completion rate)
- Set up basic moderation bots (MEE6, Carl-bot, or AI-powered in 2026)

**Growth (Weeks 2-8):**
- Define your ideal member and attract with simple entry points (tweets, landing pages explaining one benefit)
- Founder AMAs build transparency and humanize leadership
- Token-gating for Web3: NFT-based access, staking tiers, subscription tiers unlocking DeFi perks
- Cross-platform integration: every Twitter thread, Reddit post, and blog should link to Discord

**Retention Focus:**
- Respond to every message personally (in early stage)
- Weekly AMAs or voice chats with the founder
- Community recognition: highlight top contributors, create ambassador roles
- 2026 trend: AI-powered moderation bots handle scale while maintaining quality

### Expected Results

- 100-500 members in 90 days
- But these are your highest-quality users: retention is 3-5x higher than users from launch platforms
- Discord members become your seed audience for every future launch

## 9. Referral / Affiliate Programs

**Why it works:** Turns your existing users into a distribution channel. Multiplier on all other channels. Near-zero marginal cost per acquisition.

### Implementation for Solo Developer

**Tools:**
- Rewardful ($0-49/mo, Stripe integration, purpose-built for SaaS)
- FirstPromoter ($focused on subscription economy, deep Stripe support)
- Simple custom solution: unique referral links + tracking table + manual payouts

**Commission structures that work:**
- SaaS: 20-30% recurring commission on referred subscriptions
- Crypto: Revenue share on trading fees (industry standard: 20-50%)
- Tiered: 20% for first 50 referrals, 30% at 50+, 40% at 200+

**Key 2026 insight:** Cookie-based tracking is dying (browsers block third-party cookies). Use server-side attribution or you will lose tracked referrals.

### Expected Results

- Referral programs typically deliver 2-5x multiplier on organic growth once established
- 4-8 weeks to see meaningful results
- Requires existing user base to activate (pair with other channels first)

## 10. Airdrops (Crypto-Specific)

**Why it works:** 78% of successful blockchain launches in 2026 use airdrops as a core growth strategy. Can acquire 100,000+ users for under $500,000 in token value. Far cheaper than conventional advertising.

### The 2026 "Intelligent Airdrop" Model

Old model (just distribute tokens) is dead. 88% of airdropped tokens lose value within 3 months. 60% of recipients become inactive.

**What works in 2026:**
- **Task-based verification:** Require 4-5 verifiable actions (staking, providing liquidity, using the product, social engagement)
- **Tiered rewards:** 60% of tokens to high-engagement users (5+ verifiable actions), 30% to medium, 10% to baseline
- **Sybil resistance:** Use Civic or similar identity verification. Civic's model reduced fraud by 63% while increasing qualified user acquisition by 48%
- **Retention hooks:** Vesting schedules, staking bonuses, ongoing usage rewards

### Results

- Intelligent airdrops: 73%+ retention rate (vs. 11% for standard giveaways)
- Cost: Token allocation (you decide the economic impact)
- Risk: If poorly designed, attracts mercenary capital that dumps tokens immediately
- Best when: You have a token, need rapid community bootstrap, and can design meaningful engagement requirements

## 11. Integration Partnerships

**Why it works:** Being embedded inside products people already use eliminates the discovery problem entirely. API economy in 2026: $16.29B market growing at 34% annually.

### Strategy for Solo Developer

1. **Identify 5-10 products your target users already use**
2. **Build a native integration** (Zapier, embedded widget, API connector, plugin)
3. **Get listed in their marketplace/directory** (Notion integrations, Slack App Directory, Shopify App Store, etc.)
4. **Co-marketing:** Partner announces your integration to their audience

### Examples

- Build a Zapier integration: Listed in Zapier's directory, discovered by their 2.2M+ users
- Chrome extension that enhances an existing popular tool
- VS Code extension that connects to your backend
- Telegram bot that extends an existing service

### Expected Results

- 4-12 weeks to build and get listed
- 100-500 high-quality users in first 90 days
- These users have highest LTV because they are already in a workflow where your product adds value
- Compounds over time as partner's user base grows

## 12. Direct Sales / Outbound (B2B/Enterprise)

**Why it works:** Highest LTV users. You choose exactly who to target. Works from day 1 with zero audience. Net cost per booked meeting: $0.79 with Instantly vs. $200+ with a human SDR.

### Solo Developer Outbound Stack (2026)

**Tool:** Instantly ($37/mo Growth plan, unlimited email accounts)
- Built-in lead sourcing (50M+ verified B2B contacts)
- AI-powered sequencing and follow-up
- Warm-up included

**Process:**
1. Define ICP (Ideal Customer Profile): job title, company size, industry, tech stack
2. Build targeted list (100-500 prospects per campaign)
3. Write 3-email sequence: value-first, case study, direct ask
4. Send 50-100 emails/day per warmed-up account
5. AI handles follow-up, you handle qualified meetings

**Expected results:**
- Cold email: 10-20% reply rate, 5-10% conversion
- 100 emails = 5-10 quality users
- Best for B2B/enterprise products with $100+/mo price points
- Hybrid model (AI first-touch + human meetings) beats human SDR cost by 10-50x

## Crypto Distribution vs. Traditional SaaS: Key Differences

| Dimension | Traditional SaaS | Crypto/Web3 |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| Primary channel | Google SEO + content marketing | Twitter/X + Telegram + Discord |
| User incentive | Free trial / freemium | Token incentives / airdrops |
| Community role | Nice-to-have (support) | Essential (governance, evangelism) |
| Virality mechanism | Referral programs | Token-gated access, airdrops, staking rewards |
| Trust building | Case studies, reviews, SOC2 | Open source code, on-chain transparency, audits |
| Network effects | Weak (data, integrations) | Strong (token value, liquidity, governance) |
| Retention model | Product value + switching costs | Token value + staking + community identity |
| Speed to users | Slow (months) | Fast (days via token incentives) |
| User quality risk | Low | High (mercenary capital, airdrop farmers) |
| Key 2026 shift | AI-powered outbound replacing SDRs | Moving from incentive-driven to utility-driven growth |

**The core distinction in 2026:** Crypto projects are moving away from token incentives as the primary growth driver toward genuine utility. The projects that survive combine crypto-native distribution (Twitter, Telegram, airdrops) with SaaS-quality retention (real product value, revenue models).

## Key Principles

1. **Distribution compounds when layered intentionally.** No single channel is enough. The best solo developers behave like media businesses first, product businesses second.

2. **Audience before product.** The developers who grew fastest in 2026 built an audience on Twitter by genuinely sharing what they learned BEFORE launching. Launch platforms deliver only 10-30% of your first 1,000 users.

3. **Retention beats acquisition.** 95% of users churn within 90 days for most apps. Target: 40%+ of week-1 users still active in week 4. If you do not have this, fix the product before scaling distribution.

4. **The real challenge has shifted from building to being seen.** In 2026, building is commoditized (AI-assisted). Getting noticed is the bottleneck. Plan for distribution from day 1.

5. **Dry-run, then execute.** Test your messaging in small communities before big launches. You get one shot at HN and Product Hunt -- do not waste it on untested positioning.
